TOURNAMENT TIMELINE

Every milestone from registration day to the grand finale. The road to glory.

Tentative Schedule

1 August – 15 September, 2026
Registration

Registration Opens

Fee (₹5,000)

The official team and player registration portal goes live. Submit your squad details and secure your spot in the tournament. First come, first served!

15 September – 30 September, 2026
Late Fee

Registration with Late Fee — ₹7,500

Registrations submitted on or after this date will incur a late fee of ₹7,500.

September 30, 2026
Deadline

Registration Closes

Final deadline for team submissions. Late entries will NOT be accepted.

October 2026
Auction

Franchise Teams Auction

The official franchise teams auction takes place. Teams are bid on, squads are assembled, and franchises secure their players for the tournament. A thrilling day of strategy and competition.

October 2026
Trials

Player Trials

Open player trials across Delhi/NCR venues. Aspiring cricketers showcase their batting, bowling, and fielding skills. Top performers get selected for the franchise squads.

October 2026
Auction

Players Auction

The highly anticipated players auction where franchises bid for top cricket talent. Teams strategize their budgets and build their dream squads for the tournament.

November 2026
Development

Team Development Stage

Franchises focus on squad building, strategy planning, and hosting team development workshops to prepare the squads for the upcoming season.

December 2026
League

League Stage — 12 Matches

12 high-octane T20 matches across Delhi/NCR venues. Every team fights for the top spot. Points, net run rate, and individual records all matter here.

December 2026
Semi-Finals

Semi-Finals — Top 4 Clashes

The top 4 teams from the league stage battle it out in two electrifying semi-final matches. Winners advance to the Grand Finale for the ultimate showdown.

December 2026
FINALE

Grand Finale — MCL Champion

The ultimate showdown. Two finalists. One champion. One historic night. The MCL Season 01 champion is crowned, taking home the trophy and ₹3 Crore in prize money.

Delhi/NCR
