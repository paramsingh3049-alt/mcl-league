ABOUT MCL T20

Where Cricket Meets a Cause. An initiative by Mentor & Mascot Indian Film Federation.

Mentor & Mascot Indian Film Federation

The Mentor & Mascot Indian Film Federation (MMIFF) is a premier organization dedicated to fostering talent, creativity, and development in both the sports and entertainment industries. We believe in building platforms that empower communities, support aspiring talent, and integrate corporate social responsibility with high-quality sports and cultural events.

MCL Champions League — Season 01

The MCL Champions League is MMIFF's signature sporting event, bringing together elite players, state-of-the-art stadiums, and intense competition. Season 01 features 8 powerhouse franchise teams playing 6 matches to claim the coveted championship trophy.

Beyond the boundaries, MCL supports local youth development, athlete welfare programs, and social impact projects across Delhi/NCR.

Visionaries

THE VISIONARIES BEHIND MCL T20

JITENDER SHARMA

Founder President, MCL T20

Jitender Sharma is a visionary entrepreneur, and creative leader serving as the Chairperson of the Mentor & Mascot Indian Film Federation (MMIFF). He is the Founder of NXT BIG LEAP, a transformative platform that brings together artistic expression, innovative thinking, entrepreneurship, and soul-centered purpose. With over two decades of experience across media, entertainment, branding, and strategic leadership, he is committed to empowering creators, fostering meaningful collaborations, and building impactful ecosystems for the creative industry.

NARESH GUPTA

Patron, MCL T20

Naresh Gupta is a renowned entrepreneur and global business leader, serving as the President of the Indo China Chamber of Commerce and Chairman of the Indo Canada Chamber of Commerce. He is also the Founder of Tarang Foundation, driving impactful social initiatives while fostering international trade, strategic collaborations, and sustainable economic growth.

League Commissioner, MCL T20

The League Commissioner for MCL T20 will be responsible for overseeing the overall operations, rules enforcement, and strategic direction of the league. More details will be shared soon.

DR. S. KRISHNA KUMAR (Retd. I.A.S.)

League Chancellor, MCL T20

Dr. S. Krishna Kumar (Retd. I.A.S.) is a distinguished administrator and former Union Minister who has led key ministries including Information & Broadcasting, Petroleum & Natural Gas, Defence, Non-Conventional Energy, Agriculture, Health & Family Welfare, and Textiles. He also served as the Former President of the Mentor & Mascot Indian Film Federation (MMIFF) from 2021 to 2024, contributing significantly to the organization's growth and vision through his vast administrative and leadership experience.

UTTAM MAZUMDAR

Director General - Sports, MCL T20

Uttam Mazumdar is a highly respected cricket coach and mentor with over 18 years of experience in player development and high-performance coaching. A mission-driven professional, he has mentored Indian star cricketer Ishan Kishan and coached more than 20 national and international cricketers, including Rinku Singh, Dhruv Jurel, Divyansh Joshi, Keshav Dalal, Karan Sharma, Madhav Kaushik, Subh Sharma, Shoun Roger, Prateek Vats, and Sameer (Nepal). Renowned for nurturing U-16 and U-19 talent, he was appointed as Selector & Head Coach of the Big Cricket League (BCL) 2024 and also served as the Head Coach & Mentor of Team Punjab De Sher in the Celebrity Cricket League (CCL) 2024, earning recognition for his excellence in talent identification, player development, and cricket coaching.
