MMIFF Champions League T20 (MCL T20)
Cricket • Cinema • Community • Impact

MMIFF Champions League T20 (MCL T20) is a new-age cricket and entertainment platform designed to bring together competitive T20 cricket, cinema, celebrity engagement, digital innovation and social impact under one powerful league ecosystem.

With 08 men's & 08 women's franchise teams, overall 30 matches and a high-energy entertainment format, MCL T20 is built to create lasting value for fans, players, franchise owners, brands, partners and communities—on and beyond the field.

SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

MCL T20 — CRICKET FOR A CAUSE

Every Boundary Can Create an Impact.

MCL T20 is committed to transforming cricket into a platform for meaningful social change. A significant portion of franchise and sponsorship proceeds is envisioned to support initiatives across:

🩺

Sportsperson Welfare

Scholarships, training opportunities, healthcare support and health-insurance assistance for deserving and underprivileged athletes.

🎨

Artist Empowerment

Grants, platforms and opportunities for emerging filmmakers, musicians, performers and creative professionals.

🌱

Grassroots Development

Support for academies, rural tournaments, youth development programmes and cultural initiatives.
